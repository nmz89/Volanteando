# Volanteando/Wheelin'

My first Unity project: a top-down endless driving game with Flappy Bird-style controls. Your car drives forward on its own, and you press **Space** to swerve up while gravity pulls you back down. Dodge traffic and road hazards for as long as you can.

## Features

- Four difficulty modes: Easy, Normal, Hard and Impossible
- The game gets faster the longer you survive
- Best score saved separately for each difficulty
- Traffic with random speeds and spacing, plus road hazards
- Options menu with music and sound effects volume, and toggles for curb collisions and road hazards
- Available in **Spanish** and **English**

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Space or Click | Swerve up |

## Built with

Unity 6.5 (URP)
